## Overview

- One of the hardest day hikes in the world: 10,000 feet of climbing.
- Point to point: hike up the Skyline Trail to the summit, down to the tram station, take the tram down, then ride-share back.
- Something I would need to train for and be comfortable navigating in the dark.
- [Trail guide](https://hikingguy.com/hikes/cactus-to-clouds/)

## When to go

- Check closures on the park's [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mountsanjacintosp/).
- About 3–5°F cooler for every 1,000 ft you climb.

## Permits

Self-issue permit at the Long Valley Ranger Station.

## Route

- **Start to Long Valley** (10 mi, 8,000+ ft of climbing): the toughest part of the hike. If you're tired at the end of it, you can end early and take the tram down.
- **Long Valley to summit** (4.5 mi, 2,000+ ft): up in the pines, not as steep. Round Valley Campground's toilets are the last before the summit; scramble the last few hundred feet.
- **Summit to tram** (5.5 mi): backtrack to the ranger station, return the permit, then take the paved path to Mountain Station. Can seem longer and tougher than an average downhill.

## Pacing

- **Pace calculator**: the [trail guide's calculator](https://hikingguy.com/hikes/cactus-to-clouds/#pace-calculator) builds a timetable with estimated arrival times and temperatures at every key landmark.
- **Start time**: Start in the dark (often 2–4am). The trail below Grubbs Notch (8.5 miles) gets sun by mid-morning and bakes the rest of the day.
- **Heat**: Do not do the hike if the estimated temperature is 90°F+ anywhere below Grubbs Notch when you will be there.
- **Gut checks**: at the picnic tables, then again at Rescue 1. If you're tired, having doubts, or the conditions feel off, turn around. In hot conditions, make that call at the picnic tables.
- **After Rescue 1**: don't turn around and descend through the heat; continue up to Long Valley. Hikers have gotten lost going down and run out of water and time.
- **Tram cutoff**: make sure your projected end time isn't after the last tram of the night.

## Tram

- Buy a one-way ticket down at the Mountain Station gift shop (cash or credit). Mountain Station has food, drink and indoor seating.
- Last trams typically leave in the evening; confirm the [schedule](https://pstramway.com/about-us/hours/).
- Tram is closed around September for about a month for maintenance.

## On-trail navigation

- Going off trail can get you cliffed out easily.
- Checking the GPX on your phone frequently is necessary.
- Carry redundant GPS with the GPX loaded.
- Local hikers blaze white dots, but they fade, so don't rely on them alone.

## Training

Get used to climbing for 8–12 hours straight and hiking 12–16 hours. Mt Whitney could be a warmup.
