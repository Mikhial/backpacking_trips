## Overview

- Best close solo option, and the highest-rated close option
- Call at least 2 days ahead for a camp reservation. Shouldn't be difficult to get one.
- 45 minutes to 1.5 hours depending on traffic, plus a 45-minute Uber to the other side
- 28 miles, 7,500 ft of gain, 1–2 nights

## Route

[Mission Peak to Del Valle](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/ohlone-wilderness-regional-trail-del-valle-to-mission-peak): Point to point.

- Park at Mission Peak and Uber to Del Valle (east to west)
- [Reddit trip report](https://www.reddit.com/r/norcalhiking/comments/1iwpolp/solo_backpacking_the_ohlone_wilderness_trail_28/)

## Permits and reservations

- Trail permit dropped as of Jan 2026. Camps are reserved by phone, at least 2 business days ahead. [Reservation site](https://www.reserveamerica.com/explore/sunol/EB/110028/overview)
- $15/site/night + $8 reservation fee. Maximum 3 nights.
- Reservations are made by calling the EBRPD Reservations Department. Maximum 2 reservations per call.
- Group and backpack sites are released in two six-month blocks. On opening dates, reservations are taken by phone only.
  - Arrival dates July–December 2026 are open now.
  - Arrival dates January–June 2027 open in December 2026.

## Camps

Details: [EBRPD backpack camping FAQ](https://www.ebparks.org/recreation/camping/backpack-camping-faqs#sunol)

- **Maggie's**, 11 miles out: 3 different camps, in trees
- **[Sunol Backpack Camp](https://www.redwoodhikes.com/EastBay/SunolCG.html)**, 17 miles out: seven widely separated sites. You can't see the other sites, but you can still hear people. From almost every site it's a long, steep walk to the outhouse.
  - **Sky Camp**: ¼ mile to outhouse, good view, no picnic table, some shade, not very sheltered from wind
  - **Cathedral**: can be buggy, no view but in trees, outhouse is in the campsite so the neighboring camp walks through it, sheltered from wind
  - **Oak View**: no picnic table, not popular because of the long walk to the outhouse (or through Cathedral), no ground level
  - **Star's Rest**: large campsite, ⅕ mile to outhouse, picnic area is shaded but the tent site isn't
  - **Eagle's Aerie**: most popular; on a high ridge with a rock formation and a big oak tree, decent sized
  - **Sycamore**: not a great option
  - **Hawk's Nest**: less privacy (on the main trail), but close to the outhouse, shady, limited view
- Amenities: tables, chemical/pit toilets, non-potable water

## When to go

- Nov–May overall (green Feb–Apr); Jun–Sep is hot and shadeless.
- March to May is probably peak.
- Fall/winter is doable. Fall will be dry; winter will be cold, dark and wet.
- Jan/Feb: lows likely in the 30s, highs in the 50s. Roughly a 25% chance of rain on any given day, but rain comes in bunches with storm systems: mostly rainy for a week or so, then clear for a while. Short days.
- Seasonal springs at camps were all flowing as of Oct 16, 2026; they can dry up in late summer and fall.

## Hazards

Heat, ticks, rattlesnakes, cattle, poison oak in canyons.

## Character

Grassy ridges and oak canyons; tule elk and eagles.
