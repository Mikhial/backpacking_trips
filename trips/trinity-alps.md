## Overview

- Big: more than 550 miles of hiking trails
- Home to dozens of alpine lakes and several miles of crystal blue rivers and creeks
- Lots of trailheads all around the wilderness (35+ on the map), so lots of route options

## Permits

A wilderness permit is needed to camp overnight, any time of year, plus a California campfire permit, which also covers stoves. Neither has a quota or reservations.

- **Wilderness permit**: at outdoor kiosks at the Weaverville Ranger Station (360 Main St), the Supervisor's Office in Redding (3644 Avtech Pkwy), the Shasta Lake Ranger Station, and the Coffee Creek, Mule Creek (Hwy 3), Junction City and Big Bar (Hwy 299) fire stations.
- **Campfire permit**: free, [online](https://permit.preventwildfiresca.org/) or at any Forest Service, BLM or CAL FIRE office.

**Camping**: up to 14 nights in 30 days; groups of 10 or fewer. Bear canister required in the Canyon Creek and Swift Creek drainages; elsewhere, any bear-proof system. Wood fires are banned in parts of the wilderness, even with a campfire permit (stoves are fine); see the [restrictions](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/shasta-trinity/alerts/trinity-alps-wilderness-area-restrictions).

## Routes

There are other routes, but these are some of the more popular and maintained ones.

- [Stuart Fork to Emerald and Sapphire Lakes](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/us/california/stuart-fork-trail-to-emerald-and-sapphire-lakes): A long, gentle river valley through forest and mining history, ending at two big granite-cirque lakes under the Sawtooth Ridge. Caribou Lake is also a good option.
- [Canyon Creek and Boulder Creek Lakes](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/us/california/canyon-creek-and-boulder-creek-lakes): Waterfalls and granite slabs climbing to the lakes, plus a rugged off-trail side trip. The most scenic per mile, and the most crowded.
- [Four Lakes and Siligo Peak Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/us/california/four-lakes-and-siligo-peak-loop): A steep climb to the high country, then ridge walking between small alpine lakes, with a summit view of the whole range and Mt. Shasta.
- [Caribou Lakes Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/caribou-lakes-trail)
